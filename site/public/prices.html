The mix. Cache hits are the share of prompt tokens read from the cache; output is the share of all tokens written back. The rest of the prompt is new input. The three always add to 100%.

Cache writes. OpenAI and Anthropic bill a write at 1.25× input; DeepSeek and GLM charge none, so a written token costs their input price. Anthropic is the only vendor with a published 1-hour write (2× input) — elsewhere “1 hour” is the 5-minute price.

Long prompts. A vendor bills the whole request at the tier its prompt reaches: above 272K on OpenAI, 100K on Claude Haiku 5.5, 32K on GLM. Move “Prompt per request” to see it.

Service tiers. Batch bills at half the standard price where a vendor runs one — OpenAI, Anthropic and GLM. DeepSeek's discount is off-peak hours (a schedule, not shown) and Moonshot lists none, so those stay at standard under Batch. Priority is a rate-limit tier, not a price, so it isn't here. Also not counted: subscription plans, tool, network and storage fees, and tokenizer differences — the same text is about 30% more tokens on Claude 4.7 and later.

Source. List prices from models.dev, read on —. A negotiated price or a plan can differ; magpie-friday’s Usage page prices what you actually spent.
